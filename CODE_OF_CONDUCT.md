# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Luraba welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive
feedback, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publication of private
information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to [lamongabriel@icloud.com](mailto:lamongabriel@icloud.com).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may
correct behavior, issue warnings, remove contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban participants when necessary.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
